BILL OF THE DAY : SJRES.181
Senate rejects motion to force SJRes. 181 out of committee by a vote of 47–53.
S.J.Res. 181 would direct the removal of U.S. Armed Forces from hostilities within or against Iran that have not been authorized by Congress. The failed procedural vote means the resolution remains in the Senate Foreign Relations Committee.
