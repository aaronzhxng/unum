BILL OF THE DAY : SJRES.181
Rep. Jason Smith's AGOA Extension Act passes the House 340–54 and heads to the Senate.
H.R. 6500 would extend duty-free trade benefits for eligible sub-Saharan African countries under the African Growth and Opportunity Act (AGOA) through 2028. The bill also extends customs user fees through 2031.
