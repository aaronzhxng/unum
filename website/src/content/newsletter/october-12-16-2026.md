Good morning. Neither chamber passed a bill last week, but 3 bills became law: the Illegal Red Snapper and Tuna Enforcement Act, the Crow Tribe Water Rights Settlement Amendments Act, and the Retire through Ownership Act.

Another 12 bills passed in September were sent to the President, including the Romance Scam Prevention Act and a bill making Arizona's Chiricahua National Monument a national park. 56 bills were introduced.
