Good morning. The Senate took two big votes on Thursday. It passed a broad energy and permitting package, 62 to 33, that changes how federal agencies review power lines, energy projects, and mines, and limits lawsuits against approved projects. Because the Senate rewrote a House bill to carry it, the package now goes back to the House. The same day, the Senate rejected a House-passed resolution directing the President to withdraw U.S. forces from hostilities with Iran, 48 to 51. Republican Sens. Susan Collins, Lisa Murkowski, and Rand Paul voted for it, and Democratic Sen. John Fetterman voted against it.

No bills were signed into law. Three went to the President, including the annual cost-of-living raise for veterans' disability benefits. 8 more passed both chambers, among them bills on organ transplant access for people with disabilities, e-bike battery safety, and professional boxing. 61 bills came out of committee, and 235 bills and resolutions were introduced.
