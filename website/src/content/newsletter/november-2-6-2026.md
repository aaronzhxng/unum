Good morning. On Tuesday, Election Day, the President signed the two bills that were on his desk in our last issue: one renewing ALS research programs, and one renewing the U.S. Commission on International Religious Freedom.

Neither chamber passed a bill last week. Two bills that passed in September went to the President, House committees reported 11 bills, and 14 bills and resolutions were introduced. Government funding still runs out December 11.
