---
title: How the budget is made
summary: The annual budget process: President's proposal, budget resolution, and appropriations.
---

The annual budget process begins when the President sends a budget proposal to Congress. This proposal is not binding, but it sets the terms of the debate. Congress then passes a "budget resolution" that sets overall spending limits. Finally, Congress passes a series of appropriations bills that actually authorize the spending.

If Congress fails to pass a budget, the government can shut down, but temporary funding bills called "continuing resolutions" are often used to avoid this.
