---
title: Who introduces a law?
summary: How bills are drafted, introduced, and assigned to committees.
---

A draft law is called a bill or a resolution: they are basically the same.

Any member of Congress can introduce a bill: the first person to do so becomes its "sponsor", and those who follow are called "co-sponsors". Once it is introduced, the bill gets the letters H.R. or S (standing for House of Representatives or Senate), or H.J.Res. or S.J.Res. (if it is a joint resolution), followed by a number.

The bill is then referred to one or more committees with just a few special members of the House or the Senate, who will research and debate the details of the bill and make any necessary changes.
